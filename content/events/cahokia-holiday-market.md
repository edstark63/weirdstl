The Cahokia Mounds Holiday Market runs Friday through Sunday, November 27 to 29, from noon to 5 p.m. each day. It is free. Indigenous artisans sell handcrafted jewelry, paintings and other items.

<!--more-->

It is held at Cahokia Mounds State Historic Site in Collinsville, Illinois, which makes it one of the few holiday markets in the area set beside a city that is a thousand years old. Check the site’s events listing for any changes.
