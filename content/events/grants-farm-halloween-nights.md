Grant’s Farm is running Halloween Nights from October 1 through October 30, with a haunted tram ride, shows, scare zones and photo ops, according to Explore St. Louis’s Halloween guide.

<!--more-->

The farm began as the Busch family’s private deer park, so the haunted tram has a lot of history to work with. Our story on [Grant’s Farm](/posts/grants-farm-history/) covers how it got here. The guide doesn’t list prices or hours, so check before you go.
