City Museum is running Fright at the Museum from September 30 through November 1, with what Explore St. Louis’s Halloween guide describes as heart-pounding performances, exhibits and themed bars.

<!--more-->

City Museum is already a building made of salvaged parts and a great many tunnels, which gives Halloween a head start. The guide doesn’t list prices, so check the museum’s site before you go.
