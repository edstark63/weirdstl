The Lemp Mansion is hosting a Halloween trivia competition on Sunday, October 18. The prize, according to Explore St. Louis’s Halloween guide, is an overnight stay.

<!--more-->

That is a trivia prize with a catch if you have read [our story on the mansion’s ghosts](/posts/lemp-mansion-ghosts/). The mansion is for sale but scheduled to keep operating through December 2027. The guide doesn’t list the time or the price.
